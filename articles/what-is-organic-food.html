What Does Organic Food Mean?

Organic food is grown and processed without synthetic pesticides, artificial fertilizers, chemical preservatives, or genetically modified organisms (GMOs).

Our Clean Ingredient Standard

In our organic snacks store, every ingredient—from organic tomatoes and millets to jaggery and spices—is sourced from soil free of chemical sprays for years. This ensures pure taste and safety for your family.
