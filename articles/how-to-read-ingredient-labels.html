How to Read an Ingredient Label

Understanding food labels is the single best way to protect your family from hidden chemicals and cheap filler oils.

Red Flags on Snack Labels

  • Palm oil or vegetable fat: Often hydrogenated and highly processed.
  • INS numbers (E-numbers): Synthetic preservatives like INS 211 (Sodium Benzoate).
  • High Fructose Corn Syrup: Cheap artificial sweetener used in commercial ketchups.
