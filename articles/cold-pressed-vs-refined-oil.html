Cold-Pressed Oil vs Refined Oil

Cold-pressed (wood-pressed) oil is extracted naturally by crushing seeds at low temperatures without chemical solvents like hexane.

Why We Use Organic Cold-Pressed Oils

Refined oils undergo high-temperature heating and bleaching that destroy natural antioxidants and create trans-fats. Our oil-based snacks use only certified organic cold-pressed oils extracted using traditional wood-pressing methods.
